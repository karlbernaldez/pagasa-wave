## Decision evidence record

When a decision is made, update the corresponding row with:

- decision outcome and conditions;
- approving name/role;
- authority reference such as memorandum, meeting minutes, ticket, or signed review;
- decision date and effective date;
- assigned owner and due date for conditions; and
- affected requirements, ADRs, issues, and release scope.

No blank signatures are required to review this register. Formal signatures or authority references are added only after the organization selects the responsible people.
