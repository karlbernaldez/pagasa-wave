# Chart Interpretation Guide

## Purpose
This document helps the chatbot explain forecast chart interpretation concepts.

## Common Questions

### Question: How do I interpret a forecast chart?
Answer:
Interpretation depends on the chart type, symbols, annotations, and forecast data being represented.

### Question: What do markers mean?
Answer:
Markers represent operational forecast symbols or annotated marine conditions.

## Operational Interpretation Concepts

### Marker Symbols
Markers may represent:

- tropical cyclone or typhoon
- low pressure area
- high pressure area
- wave condition indicators

### Annotations
Annotations may include:

- wave boundaries
- directional indicators
- operational notes
- chart overlays

### Diff View
Question: What does diff view show?
Answer:
Diff view compares historical and current project versions.

### Preview View
Question: What does preview show?
Answer:
Preview shows the current saved project representation.

## Chatbot Rules
The chatbot should avoid claiming exact operational meaning for symbols unless explicitly documented.
